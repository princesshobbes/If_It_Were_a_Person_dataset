# Changelog

## 2.0 (November 12, 2026)

The same test carried to eight more legal systems and to the home country of incidents outside the United States.

- Eight legal systems added: the European Union, the United Kingdom, Australia, India, China, Canada, South Korea, and South Africa. Each incident is answered for each of them under the law today and under the law in force on the incident date.
- 288 incidents that happened in one country outside the United States also carry a home country reading.
- 872 laws added to the law library (559 from the eight systems, 313 from 62 home countries), each with where it was read and what was read.
- 799 rows added to the precedents: one search per law outside the United States, 367 of them with a court case or regulator action on record in which the law was applied, and 90 more where the decision found did not apply it (an acquittal, a conviction set aside, a claim that failed, a provision struck down). The second group is labeled and not counted as found.
- A new answer, Administrative penalty, for regulator fines and sanctions outside the United States; and two markers of an incomplete reading, Could not check and Needs a single read.
- Six supporting sheets added after the seven sheets of version 1.0.
- Every sheet and column of version 1.0 is kept; new columns sit to the right and new rows below. Four version 1.0 cells in the precedents were corrected: the case name for 421 F. App'x 11 (2d Cir. 2011) now reads United States v. Wells, the caption of that summary order (it read United States v. Gray, the caption of the companion opinion at 642 F.3d 371), and the source link for the 2020 OSHA citation of Ajin USA now points to the Department of Labor's copy of the same news release, because the earlier address stopped opening, and the pin cite and quote pin for DOT Order 2023-12-11 (Southwest Airlines) now give page 11 for ordering paragraph 2 (they gave page 12; the paragraph quoted is on page 11 of the order). Every other version 1.0 value is unchanged.
